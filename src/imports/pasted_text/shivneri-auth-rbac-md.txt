18. RESOURCE OWNERSHIP
============================================================

RBAC alone is NOT enough.

Add ownership rules.

Employee:

Can view own rides.

Employee cannot view another employee's ride.

Driver:

Can view assigned rides.

Driver cannot access another driver's trips.

Manager:

Can access employees within assigned organization / department / scope.

Example:

ALLOW:

ride.employee_id == user.id

AND

ride.organization_id == user.organization_id

Otherwise:

DENY.

This follows the documented resource ownership model. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}
